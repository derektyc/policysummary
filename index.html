Protection Summary

Hospitalisation is treated as “As Charged” when an as-charged plan/rider is present; otherwise ward-class coverage is shown as non-as-charged. Coverage figures are summed across policies.

Wealth Summary

Wealth Summary focuses on savings, investment and retirement policies. Any protection benefits attached to a wealth policy are shown in Remarks and still count toward Protection Gap Analysis.

Overview

Protection Gap Analysis

Default recommendation logic: Emergency Fund = 6× monthly income; Death / TPD / Accident / Major CI = 10× annual income (minimum $500,000); Early CI = 5× annual income (minimum $300,000); Retirement Lump Sum = 8× annual income (minimum $500,000). Retirement Income is shown separately based on Desired Monthly Retirement Income and Monthly Expenses; both are projected to the desired retirement age using 4% annual inflation.

What’s New

V2.1

  • Google Login / Drive-ready cloud autosave with persistent-session reconnect and local fallback.
  • Claims remaining-benefit indicator; AMR and TCM/Chiro claim types.
  • Automatic premium-paid and average annual return notes for investment/endowment policies.
  • Surrender value date, age-at-policy-date display, preserved remark line breaks, and improved name capitalization.
  • Compact/Split premium view (Cash: Monthly/Annual/Single; CPF: Annual/Single), persistent across sessions and respected by PDF export.
  • Protection and Wealth summaries now default to the current client’s own coverage and include linked policies owned under another profile when the current client is the insured person.
  • More compact summary rows; centred summary-table content and equal Cash/CPF premium block widths in both Compact and Split views.

  • Wealth-purpose policies now remain in Wealth Summary; attached protection benefits appear in Remarks and still count toward gap analysis.

  • Added advanced Proposal Builder with Option A / B comparison, gap integration and designed PDF proposal timelines.
  • Smart Import Policy now starts minimized, and PDF previews use a compact scrollable viewer.

  • Added per-client To-Do lists with due dates, completion tracking and notification reminders.
  • Policy edits from Protection / Wealth Summary now open in a wider in-place pop-up.
  • New-policy saving now resets the form and focuses Insurer for faster entry.
  • Notifications now include To-Dos, read controls and top-positioned panels.
